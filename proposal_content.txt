1. Introduction
Hackathons have become a cornerstone of student innovation, but most follow a familiar single-day or 24-hour format that, while exciting, often leads to burnout, rushed solutions, and limited time for genuine problem-solving. The Hackathon Premier League (HPL) reimagines this experience by combining the structure, drama, and sustained excitement of a sports league — modelled on the Indian Premier League (IPL) — with the technical depth of a hackathon.
Instead of a single intense session, HPL unfolds over three weeks as a ‘season’. Teams (referred to as Squads) compete in weekly head-to-head ‘match days’, progressively building a solution to a problem statement in structured phases, while climbing a live points table. The season culminates in an offline Grand Finale, where the top-performing squads face off for the HPL Championship title.
This format keeps participants engaged over a longer period, encourages iterative and well-thought-out development (similar to agile sprints), and creates the kind of anticipation, rivalry, and celebration typically associated with a sporting league — making the event memorable for participants, judges, and the institution alike.

3. Concept Overview — The HPL Analogy
The core idea behind HPL is to translate the season-long, competitive, and entertainment-driven structure of the IPL into a technical event. The table below maps familiar IPL elements to their corresponding HPL components, giving stakeholders an immediate sense of how the format works.

4. Objectives
The Hackathon Premier League aims to:
Create a sustained, multi-week engagement model that keeps students invested in problem-solving over time, rather than a single high-pressure day.
Encourage iterative, sprint-based development by splitting each problem statement into three progressive phases.
Introduce a fair, transparent, and exciting evaluation system through head-to-head matches and a live points table.
Foster healthy competition, team spirit, and strategic thinking similar to a sports league environment.
Provide participants with continuous feedback from judges across multiple rounds, improving learning outcomes.
Build anticipation and a sense of community through weekly ‘match day’ updates, leaderboards, and a high-energy offline finale.
Position the institution as an innovator in event formats, offering a unique showcase for sponsors, faculty, and media.
5. Event Structure

Every problem statement is divided into three progressive phases — one per week — so that squads build their solution incrementally, much like a season unfolds match by match. 5.2 Team Composition — Squads
Participants will register in self-formed teams called Squads, with the following composition:
Squad Size: 5 members per squad — 4 Core Members + 1 Squad Member (an additional member who can be rotated in/out for different phases, similar to IPL’s ‘Impact Player’ concept, allowing flexibility in contribution).
Self-Formed Teams: Only the Team leader have to register with his team. Team members need not register separatly
Franchise Identity: Each squad selects a team/franchise name and (optionally) a logo adding a fun, competitive branding element throughout the season.

6. Match Format & Scoring System
Each week, squads within the same track are paired into head-to-head fixtures — similar to league match-ups. Both squads in a fixture submit their work for that week’s phase, and a judging panel evaluates the two submissions relative to each other against a common rubric (see Section 8).
6.1 Points System
Points are awarded after each head-to-head match as follows:

6.2 The Points Table
A live, cumulative Points Table is maintained for each track and updated after every match day, showing each squad’s total points, matches played, and net evaluation score (an aggregated judge score used as a tiebreaker, similar to Net Run Rate in the IPL).
At the end of Week 2, the points tables across all tracks are consolidated. The top 10 squads overall (or a defined number per track, to be finalised by the organising committee) qualify for the Grand Finale — mirroring the IPL Playoffs.
7. Grand Finale — Week 3 (Offline)
The Grand Finale brings the top 10 qualifying squads together on campus for a high-energy, in-person culmination of the season.
Squads present and demo the complete, final version of their solution (Phase 3), incorporating feedback and improvements from Phases 1 and 2.
A panel of judges — ideally including faculty, industry experts, and special guests — evaluates each presentation live.
Finale scores are combined with cumulative league points to determine the Final League Table and crown the HPL Champions.
The event will include an awards ceremony recognising the Champion Squad, Runner-Up, and special individual awards (see Section 9).

12. Expected Outcomes & Benefits
Higher sustained engagement: A 3-week season generates ongoing buzz and conversation, compared to a one-day event that is quickly forgotten.
Better quality solutions: Phased development encourages iteration, feedback incorporation, and more polished final products.
Skill development: Participants experience structured, sprint-style project execution — a valuable, industry-relevant skill.
Stronger community building: Weekly matches, leaderboards, and rivalries foster a sense of community and excitement similar to following a sports league.
Enhanced institutional branding: A first-of-its-kind format positions SMVITM as an innovator in student engagement and technical events, with strong potential for media coverage and sponsorships.
13. Conclusion
The Hackathon Premier League offers a fresh, engaging, and structured alternative to the traditional one-day hackathon. By borrowing the season-long excitement, fixtures, points table, and finale drama of the IPL, HPL transforms technical competition into an experience that participants will follow, discuss, and remember — much like a sporting season.
We believe this format will not only deliver stronger technical outcomes through its phased approach, but also significantly boost student engagement, campus energy, and the institution’s reputation as a hub for innovative events. We look forward to your feedback and support in bringing the first season of HPL to life.
